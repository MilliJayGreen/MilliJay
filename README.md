# MilliJay

A little politics, a little gaming, and a lot on my mind.

MilliJay is my corner of the internet for civics, history, opinions sin filtro, gaming, and everyday life through a millennial Latina’s lens.

## What’s here

- **Civics & History:** Breaking down government, voting, and the history behind the headlines.
- **MilliJay Sin Filtro:** My opinions, my perspective, and plenty to talk about.
- **Just for Fun:** Funny stories, random thoughts, and whatever I’m into.
- **Gaming:** What I’m playing, loving, and yelling at my screen about.

## Behind the design

Purple and green, Salvadoran volcanoes, and flor de izote meet “We the People.” A reflection of my roots and my belief in freedom, civil rights, and having a voice.

Con raíces y con voz.
