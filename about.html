a little about me

An HR professional, PC gamer, college student, and millennial Latina with a lot on my mind.

I’m a first-generation Salvadoran American, born and raised in Boston. My roots shape how I see the world, and so does being raised by people who worked hard, spoke up, and showed up for their community.

I’ve spent my adult life in HR, mostly recruiting, and more recently working in operations and systems. I’m also finishing my degree in Human Resource Management. Apparently, work and school weren’t enough, because now I’ve decided to build a blog too. 😂

I created MilliJay because my interests don’t fit neatly into one category. I care about civic education, history, workers’ rights, and the decisions that affect our everyday lives. I also love PC gaming, anime, technology, and having a good laugh.

Here, you’ll find explainers, current events, stories about our culture, and my opinions sin filtro (unfiltered). I’ll share sources when discussing facts and make it clear when I’m giving you my take. You don’t have to agree with me. I just hope we leave each conversation knowing a little more than we did before.

Some days we’re talking about constitutional rights. Other days, I’m yelling at my screen over a game. There’s room for both.

Welcome to my corner of the internet.MilliJayCon raíces y con voz.
